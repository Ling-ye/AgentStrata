# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AgentStrata contributors must keep technical discussion respectful, specific,
and evidence-based. Welcoming behavior includes giving actionable feedback,
crediting others' work, respecting different experience levels, and accepting
responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in
project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `616202172@qq.com`. Include only the
minimum evidence needed; do not post personal information in a public issue.
The maintainer will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant. Knowingly false or retaliatory
reports may receive the same response.

This policy is a condensed, modified adaptation of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license. Changes include project-specific scope, reporting, and enforcement text.
Questions about an enforcement decision may be sent to the same address.
